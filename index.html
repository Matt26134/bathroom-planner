First-fix plumbing

Pipe routes & ordering

Draw separate hot, cold, mixed-water, waste and soil runs in Plan or directly in 3D. Route nodes have individual heights, so runs can travel under the floor, turn vertically through a stud/shower wall and continue at mixer or outlet height.

Routes
Move complete saved route
On the Plan, drag the coloured pipe itself to move the whole saved route in X/Y. These controls nudge the complete route without changing its shape.
Vertical movement shifts every node together, preserving risers, drops and slopes.
Route heights / wall risers
Each route point can have its own Z height. Use Add riser/drop to insert a second point at exactly the same plan position, then set its target height. That creates a true vertical pipe up/down a wall.
Ordering summary
Z can be negative for under-floor routes. For a wall riser, add a riser/drop at the wall point and give the new node the mixer/head/basin height. Waste/soil fall is calculated from the first to last route height against horizontal route length. This is a planning/ordering aid; confirm pipe sizing, falls and fitting requirements before installation.
